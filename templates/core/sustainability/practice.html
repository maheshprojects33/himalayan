In practice

What we actually do

Tree-free paper

Lokta bark is cut above the root so the shrub regenerates in 4–6 years. No trees are felled for our paper.

Natural fibres

Cashmere is combed, not sheared, from herds managed by high-altitude communities we buy from directly.

Low-impact dyes

Azo-free and natural dyes, with water treated before it leaves our units.

Fair wages

Above-market piece rates, on-time payment, and no child labour anywhere in our supply chain.

Women's employment

More than half of our workforce is women, many in supervisory and quality roles.

Minimal waste

Paper offcuts become gift tags and pulp; metal scrap is recast; herbal residue is composted.

Plastic-free packing

Paper, cloth and recycled card wherever the freight mode allows it.

Skills training

Annual workshops so young artisans learn techniques that would otherwise disappear.

Long-term buying

Year-round orders to partner workshops rather than seasonal boom and bust.
