Our commitment

A promise to our forests and our people

Sustainability is not a marketing line for us — it is the condition that lets a 25-year-old craft business keep operating in the same valleys with the same families.

We harvest lokta on a rotation that lets the shrub regrow, buy fleece and herbs at prices that keep highland communities viable, use low-impact dyes, and reuse or recycle process water and offcuts wherever we can.
