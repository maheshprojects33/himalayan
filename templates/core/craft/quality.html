Quality

Five checkpoints before a carton is sealed

01

Raw material

Bark, fleece, metal and herbs are graded on arrival and rejected if they miss our standard.

02

In-process

Supervisors check gsm, weave density, casting finish and moisture at each stage.

03

Sample match

Bulk output is compared piece by piece against your approved reference sample.

04

Final inspection

Counting, labelling, barcodes and finish checked before packing.

05

Packing

Moisture-safe, export-grade packing designed to survive long sea journeys.

06

Documentation

Invoice, packing list, certificate of origin and any product certificates prepared with the shipment.
