Pashmina

Chyangra fleece, hand-loomed

Our pashmina uses fine undercoat fleece from the Chyangra mountain goat of the high Himalaya, combed by hand each spring.

The fibre is de-haired, spun and dyed, then woven on handlooms in the Kathmandu Valley. Finishing — washing, brushing, fringing and pressing — is done by hand, which is why a real pashmina drapes the way it does.

  • 100% cashmere and cashmere-silk blends
  • Custom colours matched to Pantone references
  • Shawls, stoles, scarves and wraps in your sizes
