Herbals

Highland herbs, small-batch processing

Nepal's altitude range grows an unusual variety of medicinal and aromatic plants, from tulsi and lemongrass in the mid-hills to juniper and rhododendron higher up.

We work with collector groups and organic farms, then dry, distil and blend in small batches at our processing unit so aroma and potency survive the journey to your market.

  • Herbal teas, essential oils, balms, incense and soaps
  • Bulk supply or retail-ready packaging
  • Lab test reports and documentation available
