Statues

Lost-wax casting from Patan

Statue making in Patan is a Newar tradition passed through families for generations, and our casting studio works the same way.

A wax model is sculpted, coated in clay, fired so the wax runs out, and molten copper or brass is poured into the cavity. The raw casting is then chiselled, polished, gilded and hand-painted — a single piece can take weeks.

  • Copper, brass and bronze; gold gilding on request
  • Buddha, Tara, Ganesh, Manjushri and custom deities
  • Heights from 4 inches to life-size commissions
