Handmade paper

Lokta bark to finished sheet

Lokta is a Himalayan shrub that regenerates from its own rootstock within a few years of harvesting — which makes our paper genuinely tree-free.

The bark is cooked, washed and beaten to a pulp, then poured onto a fine cotton screen floated in water. Each sheet is lifted, drained and dried in the sun on wooden frames, giving lokta paper its characteristic fibrous strength and soft texture.

  • Tree-free, naturally acid-free and long lasting
  • 20–120 gsm, natural, dyed, printed or marbled
  • Converted into journals, wrap, bags, boxes and stationery
