Our craft

Slow processes, kept deliberately by hand

Lokta bark is harvested sustainably and cooked, beaten and cast sheet by sheet. Pashmina is hand-loomed. Statues are cast one at a time in the lost-wax method. Herbs are dried and processed in small batches.

Because the work is manual, no two pieces are identical — and that is exactly what our buyers come to Nepal for.
