Message from our CEO

“Every product we ship carries someone's name”

When you hold a sheet of our lokta paper, you are holding a hillside, a season, and a pair of hands. That is the part I never want us to lose.

I grew up around this work. In Nepal, craft has never been an industry first — it is something families do, passed from one generation to the next, in courtyards and back rooms and village workshops. When we started Himalayan Network, the ambition was simple: give that skill a fair market beyond our borders, without changing what makes it worth buying.

That is why we make everything ourselves. It would be easier and cheaper to trade goods from elsewhere, but then I could not stand behind them. Our paper is cast sheet by sheet, our pashmina is woven on handlooms, our statues are cast one at a time in the old Patan method, and our herbs are dried in small batches. It is slower. It is also the reason our buyers stay with us for years.

To every partner reading this: when you place an order, you are not only buying a product. You are keeping a craft alive and putting income into hill communities that have very few other options. We take that responsibility seriously, and we would be glad to show you exactly how your order is made — you are always welcome in our workshops.

Surendra Shakya Chief Executive Officer
