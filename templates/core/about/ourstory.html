Our story

Rooted in Nepal, trusted abroad

Himalayan Network began as a small handmade paper workshop in the Kathmandu Valley. As buyers asked for more of Nepal's crafts, we grew — adding pashmina weaving, metal statue casting and a herbal processing unit.

Today we are a single point of contact for buyers who want authentic Nepali products without juggling four suppliers. Our team manages design, production, quality control, documentation and freight, so a customer in Berlin or Boston deals with one company and one invoice.

We remain deliberately mid-sized. Growing slowly lets us keep the same artisans, the same standards and the same relationships year after year.
