What guides us

Our values

01

Honest making

Handmade means handmade. We do not mix in machine-made goods or relabel imported stock.

02

Fair livelihoods

Artisans are paid fairly and on time, with steady work through the year rather than seasonal spikes.

03

Care for the hills

Lokta is harvested on rotation, herbs are wild-crafted responsibly, and waste is minimised at every unit.

04

Consistency

An approved sample is a promise. Every shipment is checked against it before it leaves our warehouse.

05

Partnership

We plan capacity with our regular buyers so their peak seasons are never a scramble.

06

Transparency

Ask us where something is made and who made it — we will tell you, and often introduce you.
